FAQs

What is a hackathon?

A day where teams build a project from scratch and demo it to judges at the end. You pick the idea, you write the code, and you have a hard deadline. Most people arrive without knowing what they'll make.

Who can attend?

Any high school student. You don't need to be part of DevFintech, and you don't need to be from a specific school.

How big can teams be?

Up to four people. You can register solo and get matched into a team at check-in, plenty of winning teams meet that morning.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, a student ID, and a water bottle. If you're going for the hardware track, bring your own components.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Entry is free and both meals are catered.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. There's a workshop right after opening, mentors on the floor all day, and every team needs someone who can design the thing and pitch it.

What are the prizes?

One winner per track plus an overall winner. Specific prizes are announced as sponsors lock in, register and you'll hear first.

Is there a Discord?

Yes. The invite is in your confirmation email, and that's where every announcement goes.
