Questions

FAQ

Who can come?

Any student in grades 6–12, middle school or high school. You don't need to be part of DevFintech, and you don't need to be from a specific school.

Do I need a team?

No. Teams go up to four people, and you can register solo and get matched at check-in. Plenty of winning teams meet that morning.

Is it actually free?

Yes. Entry costs nothing and both meals are catered.

What are the tracks?

Three tracks plus an overall winner. They stay locked until the opening ceremony at 08:45 so nobody starts early — the theme drops at the same time.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, a student ID, and a water bottle. If you're planning to build anything physical, bring your own components.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Opening covers the basics, mentors are on the floor all day, and every team needs someone who can design the thing and pitch it.

Can I start building before the event?

No. That's the whole reason the tracks and theme stay hidden until the morning. Everyone starts from the same place.

Is there a Discord?

Yes. The invite is in your confirmation email, and that's where every announcement goes.
